## The single worst thing

**Getting to "Calibrate" for the cycle I was reading, on the phone.** The cycle page has no Calibrate control; the way in is one link ("OPEN IN THE CHART →") seven screens down. That link drops you on the chart page below the Facets/Calibrate tabs, so the control you came for is off-screen above you. And when you tap Calibrate, it silently switches to a different cycle (Ibn Khaldun) even though the address still says `focus=schlesinger_jr`. A first-time reader who does not notice the highlighted chip drags Ibn Khaldun's slider believing it is Schlesinger's. After re-selecting the cycle, the slider is another screen down and the r number is below that, so the curve and r are hard to watch at the same moment. It cost me 4 taps, 3 scrolls and one wrong-cycle moment for something the cycle page advertises in a single sentence. It is the worst because it is the only place where the site put me on the wrong thing without telling me; everywhere else I was slowed, here I could have been misled.

2. **Tapping the spectral figure on the phone makes it smaller, on a dead-end page.** It opens the bare SVG shrunk to the screen width, with labels around 4–6 px, no site header and no way back except the browser's Back. The tap looks like "enlarge" and does the opposite.
