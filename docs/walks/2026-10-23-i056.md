> 2. What does this card say about whether the cycles hold up?

>    peak today)? If yes, which one and what does it say?

> 5. Would you tap the link? Why or why not, in one sentence?

## Pass bar (fixed before the walk)

- **HELD** if answer 2 says, in the walker's own words, that the card's answer is no / not yet /
  none can be tested or confirmed (any wording that carries "not established"), AND answer 3 says
  the card gives no current position for any cycle.
- **NOT HELD** if answer 2 says the cycles do hold up, says the card does not answer, or cannot
  read the answer; OR answer 3 names a cycle as currently rising / peaking / falling.
- Answers 1, 4 and 5 are recorded, not scored. Answer 4 is expected to name the row labels and the
  small detail line (unreadable at 1x by the lane's own look on 2026-10-23); that is not a fail.
