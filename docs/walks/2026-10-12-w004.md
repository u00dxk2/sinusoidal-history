# W-004 cold walk, 2026-10-12

**N=1, synthetic.** One agent walked the live site with a scripted headless browser. It is not a user, and nothing here is a user's reaction. Treat each finding as a hypothesis about what a stranger might see.

- **Browser:** Playwright Chromium, headless, from `node_modules` in this repo.
- **Viewports:** phone 390x844 (step 1) and 390x664 (steps 3-7), `devices["iPhone 15"]`, isMobile, hasTouch, viewport overridden; desktop 900x900 and 1440x900, mouse.
- **Site:** https://sinusoidalhistory.com, live, read 2026-10-12.
- **What was not read:** no repo source, docs, continuity, `public/` or `.md` files; no `/llms.txt` or `.md` URL. From the 10-09 walk folder only the `.mjs` driver scripts were read, for launch and dump shape; its dumps, notes and screenshots were not opened. The 10-09 notes file was read for its format only.
- **Drivers and dumps:** `tmp/walk-2026-10-12/` (`dump.mjs`, `s2.mjs`, `s2b.mjs`, `s3.mjs`, `s5.mjs`, `s7.mjs`, `s8.mjs`, plus `*-blocks.txt`, `s2-visible.txt`, `s2-log.txt`, `s8-details.txt`, `*.png`).

## Instrument notes

- Page scroll used `scrollTo`. On desktop the figure arrows were clicked with Playwright's mouse click; on the phone the "why?" link was tapped. No real swipe was tested.
- `y` values are page y in CSS px, from `getBoundingClientRect().top + scrollY`. Screens = y / viewport height.
- Block dumps list leaf text blocks (p, li, dd, td, headings, links). A paragraph's y is its top; for a sentence deep inside a long paragraph I measured the sentence itself with a text Range where it mattered (step 8).
- Playwright refuses to click a button with `aria-disabled="true"` (it waits for "enabled"). The second right-arrow click in step 2 timed out for that reason, after the figure was already fully scrolled. That is the instrument, not the page.
- Headless Chromium draws no scrollbar track, so I cannot say whether a Windows desktop reader sees one under the figure.
- The "bare cos" check in step 6 searched `document.body.innerText` plus every `alt`, `title` and `aria-label` attribute for the word `cos` as a whole word. It does not see text inside the figure SVG, which is loaded as an `<img>`.
- The step 4 read of the chart line is by eye from a 3x screenshot (`t-phone-first.png`), with x positions converted to years on the 1600-2050 axis. It is approximate to a few years.
