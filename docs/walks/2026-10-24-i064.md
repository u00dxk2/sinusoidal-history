> 1. Drag with a finger across a short stretch of the strip near its right (recent) end, to zoom in on the last 20 or 30 years.
> 2. Then start a second drag INSIDE the grey box that is now on the strip, and drag it to the left by about the width of a fingertip or two.
> 3. Then drag once more, starting OUTSIDE the grey box.
> After each step, read everything within one screen of the strip (above and below it) without scrolling up to the chart.
>
> Report: (1) for each step, what you did and, in your own words, what the strip did; quote any text near the strip that told you; (2) without scrolling up, which years is the chart now showing after each step, and how do you know; (3) after step 2, did you know whether you had moved the box or chosen new years? Quote what told you, or say nothing did; (4) in one word, would you call the strip working, limited, or broken, and why; (5) anything confusing. Be candid; a "broken" is as useful as a "working".

## Pass bar (written before the walk)

- **HELD:** (1) after step 1 the walker says the drag was widened (or made wider) and names the chart's window or the Modern view, quoting the line under the strip; AND (3) after step 2 says the box was moved (not new years chosen), quoting the line; AND (2) names the chart's years after each step without scrolling up; AND (4) is not "broken".
- **NOT HELD:** any leg above fails, or the walker says nothing near the strip told them. Record it word for word; do not reword during the walk.
- "Limited" is a pass: the floor is deliberate. The walk asks whether the reader can tell what happened, not whether they like the floor.
