## Prompt (verbatim)

> You are a curious reader who has never seen this website. A friend sent you https://sinusoidalhistory.com and said "zoom in on 1800 to 1950 and tell me what happens to the cycles." Use Playwright (Node, `import { chromium } from "file:///C:/dev/skylark/sinusoidal-cycles/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs"`) with a 390x664 viewport, isMobile: true, hasTouch: true, deviceScaleFactor 2. Read ONLY the rendered website: never open any file in the repository, never fetch /llms.txt or any .md URL. Write your scripts under <scratch>/walk-i066/ (one command per shell call, absolute paths, no `cd`, no `&&`, no pipes). Drive touch with CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` (touchStart, several touchMove, touchEnd). Take a viewport screenshot (not full-page) before and after each drag and look at them.
>
> Load the page and find the control that changes which years are shown. Scroll ONCE so that control is on screen, at whatever position you choose. From then on, do NOT scroll during steps 1-2. Then:
> 1. Drag with a finger across the control to choose roughly 1800 to 1950.
> 2. Tap the button that shows all years again.
> After each step, look at the screenshot of what is on screen.
>
> Report: (1) where the control was relative to the chart when you found it; (2) after step 1, without scrolling, what did the chart itself do? Describe what changed in the drawing (shapes, axis years), not just any text; (3) did you have to scroll, or would you have had to, to see the chart change? (4) after step 2, what did the chart do? (5) in one word, would you call the zoom working, limited, or broken, and why; (6) anything confusing. Be candid; a "broken" is as useful as a "working".

## Pass bar (written before the walk)

- **HELD:** (2) after step 1 the walker describes a change in the drawing itself (curves widened/stretched/moved, or the axis years changed), seen in the screenshot taken without scrolling; AND (3) says no scroll was needed to see it; AND (4) describes the drawing returning; AND (5) is not "broken".
- **NOT HELD:** the walker had to scroll to see the chart, or describes only the text under the strip, or says nothing on screen changed. Record it word for word; do not reword during the walk.
- "Limited" is a pass if the reason is the 150-year floor or taste, not the distance.
