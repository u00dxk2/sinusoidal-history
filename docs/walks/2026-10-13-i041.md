## Answer to Q (written before re-reading)
"Did this site test Kondratiev's 54-year idea? What does 'No test was run' refer to?"

Not on the theory's own terms. The site pairs Kondratiev with US productivity growth (TFP), and that record is only 77 years, 1.4 of the three full 54-year periods the site demands. So the site declined to test that pairing, and "No test was run" means exactly that: no test of the 54-year period against the TFP record, so no p-value for it. Separately, as a side check, the site took the bare 54-year number and ran it against two unrelated long records (V-Dem democracy index and war deaths). Neither showed anything (p around 0.8). The site says plainly that this is not Kondratiev's claim. So the honest summary is: "the 54-year rhythm was looked for in two records it was never about and wasn't there; the record it IS about is too short to test."

## Do the two pages contradict each other?
**No, not in substance.** On a careful read every number matches: same 1.4 of 3.0, same two re-pairing series, same "none below 0.05", and both pages say the re-pairings are not the theory's claim (/methods: "not the site's claims"; Kondratiev: "none is this theory's own claim").

**But there are three places where a reader could think they do:**
1. **"No test was run and no p-value exists" (y≈2528) is followed 140px later by a paragraph that prints a p-value for the 54-year period (y≈2670, "lowest p 0.786").** It is scoped ("this record"), but that scope is carried by one word. A skimmer who reads "no p-value exists" and then sees "p 0.786" will think the page caught itself out. The /methods wording "no code path emits a p-value" below the gate has the same tension against the cross-grid p's three sentences later, but there the gate/cross-grid split is explained in between.
2. **"lowest p 0.786" vs the table's "p 0.796".** The disclosure says its headline p is the AR(1) p; the Kondratiev V-Dem row's headline is 0.796, and 0.786 is the AR(2) figure in parentheses. The Kondratiev page's "lowest p" takes the lowest across both nulls. Defensible, but on clicking through I first looked for 0.786 as a headline number, didn't find it, and briefly thought the numbers disagreed.
3. **What the cross-grid re-pairs.** /methods: it "re-pairs each period with every series". Kondratiev page: it "re-pairs parts of this theory with other pages". Only the period is borrowed, and the things it is set against are series, not "pages". The Kondratiev wording is vaguer and slightly wrong-sounding; /methods is the accurate one.

Not a contradiction but a gap: /methods says 54- and 55-year results are "one ~54–55-year statement" (Kondratiev = Perez). The Kondratiev page never mentions that its two re-pairing results are effectively the same test as Perez's.

## 2. Answers, written before re-reading anything

**Q1: Did this site test anything to do with Strauss-Howe? What was and was not tested?**

Strauss-Howe's own claim, an 84-year cycle in the V-Dem liberal-democracy record, was **not** tested. The record is 236 years, which is 2.8 periods, and the site won't run its test below 3.0. So there is no verdict and no p-value for the claim, and the page is careful to say that this is neither for nor against the theory (y 3532, y 3712).

What **was** run touches this page only through its data. The same V-Dem record was tested against six *other* theories' periods (30, 50, 54, 55, 60 and 75 years). Those are shorter, so 236 years spans enough of them to clear the gate. None came out significant; the best was p 0.094 (y 3812). The page says outright that these are not Strauss-Howe's claim. Nothing I read says whether the 84-year period itself was ever tried against any other, longer record. I inferred, and the page never states, that no record on the site is long enough (about 252 years) for that.

So: the Strauss-Howe claim was not tested. Its paired data was tested, against other people's periods.

**Q2: Does anything on this page contradict "No test was run and no p-value exists"?**

Not strictly, but it comes close enough that I hesitated. The sentence at y 3532 says "No test was run and no p-value exists".
