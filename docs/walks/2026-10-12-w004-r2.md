## Verdict on the glance

Partly. On Turchin, the page this walk was built around, the picture alone still reads as the data fitting the cycle. The measured record's dip-and-rise lines up with the sinusoid's trough and climb to the 2020 peak, and that is the most legible shape on the first screen. The hatch tells a glancing reader that the left of the timeline is empty. It doesn't tell them the fit on the right is uncounted. The correction comes only from text: the key's trailing "A shape that looks alike is not a test." and the verdict box headline. On Kondratiev and Perez the picture doesn't over-sell, because the records are visibly noisy or declining, and picture and verdict agree on "no fit". There the hatch has its own problems: a mid-timeline block that can read as a historical era (Kondratiev), and a 4.5 px sliver that's effectively invisible while the legend still names it (Perez). So the hatch works on the pages where the eye wasn't going to infer a fit anyway, and is weakest as a counterweight on the one page where it does (Turchin). Hypothesis, N=1 synthetic.
