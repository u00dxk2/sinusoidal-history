## 1a. Desktop 1440x900, /cycles/kondratiev: which TFP series was judged, and why "5-yr"?

**Did:** Loaded the page. Read the first screen, then scrolled down the page, and dumped innerText with element positions.

**Saw:** The first screen (0-900) shows the "Does it hold up?" box (y 441-755). Its body paragraph (y 524) names the series. The metadata row "PERIOD 54 YEARS · REFERENCE PEAK 1973 · PAIRED DATA TFP GROWTH · 5-YR" sits at y 892, half cut off by the bottom edge of the first screen. So the two names, "annual, unsmoothed" and "5-YR", both appear on or at the edge of screen one, and nothing near them says how they relate.

**Answer:** The verdict was judged on US TFP growth, annual and unsmoothed. The "Paired data" line says 5-yr because it names the 5-year rolling average, which is what the interactive chart draws. Tests use the annual figures because a rolling average makes neighbouring years move together, and that would distort the red-noise model the test compares against.

Sentences that gave the answer:
- y 524 (verdict box, first screen): "The record this verdict is judged on, US TFP growth (annual, unsmoothed), runs 77 years: 1.4 of the 3.0 full periods this site requires before it will run a test on a 54-year claim."
- y 2380 (Spectral verdict section, about 2.6 screens down and 1,490 px below the 5-YR label): "Why two labels: the "Paired data" line and section on this page, and the citation at the end, name the 5-year rolling average of this series, which is the version the interactive chart draws. The site measures the record's length on the annual figures, and would run any test on them, because a rolling average makes neighbouring years move together and would distort the background-noise model a test compares against."

The "which" came from screen one. The "why" needed a scroll to y ~2380. Before reaching it, I passed the "Paired data series: US TFP growth (5-yr rolling)" section (y 1854), whose description ("5-year centered rolling average of Fernald's...") does not mention the verdict.

Read alone, "99,999 bootstrap draws" sounds like something that happened to this record.
- The figure draws a multitaper spectrum with a "target: 54y" marker. It is labelled "descriptive only", but it looks like an analysis output.

**Frank note:** The answer is on the page and is clear once found. It sits 1,490 px from the label that raises the question. A reader who stops at the top sees "annual, unsmoothed" in the box and "TFP GROWTH · 5-YR" at the fold, with nothing in between to connect them.
