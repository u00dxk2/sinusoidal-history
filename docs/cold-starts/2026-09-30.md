  900-wide viewBox scales to about 0.39 on a phone, which shrinks an SVG dot to ~1.5px and
  11px SVG text to ~4px. The old reference-peak dot was nearly invisible on phones for
  exactly this reason.

  Its now-dot sits 4-5px from the reference ring on a phone. The ring is painted after

  be this lane's own command line) and stop it by PID.
