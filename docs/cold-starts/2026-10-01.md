**Non-obvious:**

- A plain `<a href="#…">` pushes a history entry with NO state, and the App Router's onPopState
  returns early on null state (`node_modules/next/dist/client/components/app-router.js:284-288`).
  That is the whole bug.
- `next/link` with a hash fixed Back but broke two native behaviours. Codex r1 found both and they
  reproduced: focus stayed at the link (Tab walked the roster above), and a second click on the same
  link did not jump. So `src/components/HashLink.tsx` does the jump by hand. Do not "simplify" it
  back to `<Link>`: the lint rule refuses that, and its message points at HashLink.
- WebKit's Tab skips links by default, so `check-verdict-reach --webkit` SKIPs the keyboard leg. A
  native `<a>` on production also reads BODY there.
- Next restores the hash scroll itself on Back. Setting `history.scrollRestoration = "manual"` does
  NOT move the landing; it was tried as a mutation and read green.
- `next start` on :3100 orphans after TaskStop, as before. Free the port by verified PID.
