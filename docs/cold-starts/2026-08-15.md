### What the design pass actually found

The editorial design is strong and **was not changed**. What sat underneath it:

- The page **scrolled sideways at 390px** (nav forced `scrollWidth` 399 > 375,
  clipping "About" off the edge).
- **`/methods` was unreachable from the nav at every width** — the link carried
  `hidden xs:inline`, but `xs` is not a configured breakpoint in this project, so the
  variant never applied and only `hidden` took effect. Latent for months.
- 32 tap targets under the 44px floor → 1. 49 uses of 9–10px text → the 11px floor.
  Focus was invisible site-wide (no `:focus-visible` rules; two components set
  `focus:outline-none`). Chart annotations 4.48:1 → 5.84:1.

### Known false positive — do not "fix" it again

The detector's **19 surviving `low-contrast` findings are a confirmed FP.** Its
pixel-sampler reads the chart gridlines/curves *behind* the SVG text, so it reports
1.1:1 for text whose real composited ratio is 5.84:1. Measured in-page, not inferred.
If a future run flags these, verify with composited-color math before touching
opacity — raising it further only adds noise to the chart. Written up for the
allowlist owner (skylark-site) in the review doc.
