### The number in this file that will lie to you

The read also shows **1481 "browser-ish"** and **778 "unknown (no UA)"** lines
(~323/day). **That is not a human audience read and must not be reported as one.**
`browser-ish` is a UA-*shape* fallback bucket — anything not a known crawler, not
empty, not our own tooling — and portfolio rule is that attribution verifies IP
ranges, never UA strings alone. On a site with zero Google presence and no inbound
links, ~323/day of browser-shaped traffic is far more likely scanners and scrapers
with spoofed UAs. **The human-residual question is OPEN**; answering it needs
reverse-DNS / IP-range work, which is a bigger change than reporting the bucket.

### Known false positive — do not "fix" it again

The detector's **19 surviving `low-contrast` findings are a confirmed FP.** Its
pixel-sampler reads the chart gridlines/curves *behind* the SVG text, so it reports
1.1:1 for text whose real composited ratio is 5.84:1. Measured in-page, not inferred.
If a future run flags these, verify with composited-color math before touching
opacity — raising it further only adds noise to the chart. Written up for the
allowlist owner (skylark-site) in the review doc.
